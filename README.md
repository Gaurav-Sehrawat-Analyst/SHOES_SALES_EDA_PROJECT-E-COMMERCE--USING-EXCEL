# SHOES_SALES_EDA_PROJECT-E-COMMERCE--USING-EXCEL

🥿 Shoes Sales Analysis (EDA Project)
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Shoes Sales data to uncover insights into customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends.
The goal is to provide actionable business intelligence for marketing, inventory management, and financial planning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📌 Conclusion
This analysis shows strong overall performance with 1,000 total orders, generating over $1.45M in revenue and an average unit price of $137.86.

Brand Insights: Nike and Adidas lead in both revenue and units sold, while Skechers and Reebok show moderate performance.

Monthly Trends: Revenue peaks in March and September, indicating seasonal demand patterns.

Sales Channels: Revenue and orders are evenly distributed across Retail Stores, Malls, and Online platforms, showing balanced channel contribution.

Customer Segmentation: Sports and running shoes dominate sales, reflecting consumer preference for activewear categories.

Geographic Reach: Multiple countries contribute to sales, with strong representation across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

✅ Key Takeaways
Top brands (Nike, Adidas) are driving profitability and should remain the focus of marketing and inventory strategies.

Seasonal spikes suggest opportunities for targeted promotions in March and September.

Balanced channel distribution indicates resilience, but optimizing online sales could further boost growth.

Product mix favors sports/running shoes, so expanding these categories may enhance revenue.
